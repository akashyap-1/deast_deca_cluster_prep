[ 81] Business plan. A business plan is the written document that is developed before starting a business. It
[ 81] explains the goals and objectives of the company and how the company intends to achieve those goals.
[ 81] Developing a business plan helps the business to be organized, understand the risks it might face, and
[ 81] develop strategies to overcome possible problems. A corporate report and an annual report are
[ 81] documents prepared to explain how a business is doing. They are often prepared for investors. A
[ 81] management plan explains the organization of a business.
